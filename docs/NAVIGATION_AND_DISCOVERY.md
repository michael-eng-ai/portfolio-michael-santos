# Navigation And Discovery

## Objective

Make the site easier to enter, easier to explore, and easier to revisit.

## Discovery Principles

- The header should expose the strongest repeat-worthy destinations
- The home page should answer:
  - why this publication matters
  - what proof exists
  - what changed recently
  - why someone should come back
- Every index page should offer a clear next step beyond passive browsing

## Home Page Content Order

1. Hero with business-facing promise and proof counters
2. Credibility strip
3. Guided entry-path section by visitor intent
4. Fresh market signals
5. Featured business cases
6. Tech Radar promotion
7. Long-form insights
8. Newsletter CTA

## Guided Entry Paths

The home page now includes a dedicated entry-path block that helps visitors choose where to start based on intent:

- market signals for freshness and repeat visits
- long-form insights for decision framing
- case studies for proof
- newsletter for recurring value

This reduces random browsing and increases the chance that first-time visitors enter the right content layer quickly.

## Detail Page Continuity

High-intent detail pages now expose a top-of-page continuity block before the main content flow.

That block should:

- show the current content layer clearly
- suggest the best complementary next step
- direct the visitor toward a repeat-worthy asset or conversion surface

## Why The Radar Matters

The `Tech Radar` is not just a side page.

It functions as:

- a repeat-worthy asset
- a differentiator from a normal portfolio
- a higher-retention bridge between content, expertise, and subscription
