# Brand Identity: Michael Barbosa Santos

## Brand Name

**Michael Barbosa Santos** — presented as an executive-facing publication on digital strategy, data platforms, and AI-driven business outcomes.

## Brand Promise

Help leaders see the connection between technology investment and measurable business results: growth, speed, trust, efficiency, and better decisions.

## Button Styles

### Primary

Gradient from Sky Light through Sky to Lavender Soft. Dark ink text. Soft glow shadow.

### Secondary

Subtle white border with near-transparent fill. Cloud text. No glow.

## Link Style

Underline animation from left using a Sky-to-Lavender gradient. Transition on hover.

## Copy Rules

- Never lead with technology names. Lead with outcomes: growth, speed, trust, clarity, advantage.
- Avoid "problem" as the first word. Use "opportunity", "challenge", "shift", or "signal".
- Headlines should feel like a promise the reader wants fulfilled.
- Supporting text should give just enough context to earn the click, not explain everything.
- Use active voice. Avoid passive constructions when possible.
- Max one idea per headline. No compound promises separated by commas.

## Tone

Confident but not arrogant. Specific but not jargon-heavy. Executive-facing but technically credible. The reader should feel they are accessing a curated, high-signal source, not a generic blog.

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not expose internal automation or pipeline logic on public pages.
- Do not use "platform", "pipeline", or "architecture" in hero-level copy unless framed as business benefit.
- Do not mix Portuguese and English within the same text block.
- Do not rely on dark-on-dark contrast. Always ensure at least 4.5:1 ratio for body text.
