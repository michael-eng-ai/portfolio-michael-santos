# Authority Profile Playbook

## Goal

Align the public profiles that support the site so distribution turns into authority, not disconnected activity.

## X Profile

- Use the same headshot, name format, and visual direction as the site and LinkedIn.
- Keep the bio outcome-oriented:
  - who you help
  - what you build
  - what signals you publish
- Add the site as the primary link.
- Pin one post that explains:
  - the business problem you solve
  - the type of proof available on the site and GitHub
  - the best first link to click

Suggested positioning:

- `Data engineering, AI, and business systems that turn operational complexity into growth.`
- `Cases, market signals, and implementation proof on michael.business and GitHub.`

## LinkedIn Profile

- Keep the headline aligned with the site title and consulting offer.
- Use the featured section for:
  - site home
  - strongest business case
  - strongest GitHub proof repo
- Use the about section to connect business value with implementation depth.

## Google Business Profile

- Use only if the consulting brand is a real business offer with a service area, contact path, and public presence.
- Prefer the consulting domain for the GBP website field.
- Add:
  - primary category close to consulting or data services
  - service list
  - business description aligned with the site
  - posts or updates that point to articles, cases, or consultations

## Wikipedia

- Treat Wikipedia as an outcome, not a marketing channel.
- Do not write or publish a self-promotional page directly.
- Only pursue it if there is independent notability and multiple reliable third-party sources.

## Content Pillars

Keep distribution focused on three repeating themes:

- business pressure and market signal
- operational implementation and GitHub proof
- consulting perspective, lessons, and decisions

## Publishing Guardrail

Every social post should answer at least one of these:

- Why does this matter to a business stakeholder?
- What operational pattern proves this is real?
- What should the reader do next on the site?
